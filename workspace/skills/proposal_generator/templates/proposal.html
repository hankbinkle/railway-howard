ZINN Architecture and Interiors (hereafter “Architect”) does not guarantee the performance of, and has no responsibility for, the acts or omissions of any contractor, subcontractor, supplier or any other entity furnishing materials or performing any work on the Project (hereafter “Project”). definitions

Terms used in this document are typically understood as follows but not all Projects will require every item listed.

  • MEP: Mechanical, Electrical, and Plumbing Engineering
  • F&F: Hard finishes, plumbing and lighting fixtures
  • Phase: Any of the project work periods noted above. They include the following and are allocated project resources according to the noted percentage: Pre-Design (PD) - 10%, Schematic Design (SD) - 40%, Design Development (DD) - 20%, Permit/Construction Drawings (CD) - 25%, Construction Administration (CA) - 5%
  • Scheduling/Schedules: Lists in table format such as the Finish and Fixture Schedule or Door Schedule.
  • Stated Construction Budget: This is the construction cost or costs range that the Client has identified as the target amount that will be set aside for the project’s completion. ZINN relies on this metric as an accurate and faithful reporting of the Client’s financial capabilities and willingness to commit such funds to the completion of the project.
  • Estimated Construction Budget: This is the estimated cost to construct the project based on current market-rate construction unit costs per square foot. The Estimated Construction Budget is not a bid for construction nor a guarantee of construction costs.

scope changes

  • This proposal is based on Stated Construction Budget and the Scope noted above.
  • In the event that the Scope and the Estimated Construction Budget do not align, either the Scope will need to be reduced to match the Stated Construction Budget or the Stated Construction Budget will need to increase to match the Scope at the completion of Schematic Design.
  • Upon a change to the Stated Construction Budget or Scope, ZINN will prepare a proposal adjustment to account for the change in work required to bring the project drawings to Permit/Construction level.
  • Any fee increases or decreases will be pro-rated over the course of the remaining payments.

engineering

If your project needs MEP and/or Structural engineering services, we solicit fee proposals from reputable consulting engineering firms to provide the required services. Once Schematic Design is complete, the Scope of the project is better defined and this is an appropriate time to solicit proposals from qualified engineers. ZINN does not charge pass-through fees or markup engineering proposals. Agreements for engineering services are contracted directly between the client and the engineer to keep the lines of communication open between all members of the design team. ZINN nonetheless will help the Client solicit such proposals and will provide engineering coordination to administer their work.

drafts

A maximum of 3 drafts will be provided for any given Deliverable (an initial issue and 2 rounds of revisions). Additional drafts may be requested and will be billed as Additional Services.

comment and production periods

To insure efficient collaboration with the Client, the Services and Deliverables noted above will be provided to the Client via Comment and Production periods for each draft. Upon issuance of a deliverable, whether via email or as a presentation at an in-person meeting, the Comment Period commences. During the Comment Period:

  • the Client provides feedback, notes, and revision requests to the Architect
  • the comments will be logged by the Architect in a Markup Copy that will show the comments in relation to the relevant areas on the Deliverable
  • the Client may change or revise their comments at any time
  • no work is undertaken by ZINN to advance the project development until the Client provides a signed Draft.

During the Production Period:

  • ZINN will process the markups in preparation for the next draft or meeting
  • We request that no additional Client comments are provided so we may be efficient about the work

Should substantive changes be requested by the Client during the Production Period, the Production Period will halt, the Comment Period will re-commence, and the current draft effort will be considered complete, counting against the number of available drafts. If the number of available drafts for the Service or Deliverable have been exhausted, the process, including both Comment and Production periods, will be undertaken as Additional Services per the hourly rates noted herein.

The Service or Deliverable will be considered complete and the process may move onto the next Services or Deliverable upon receipt by the Architect of a signed Draft with no Markups.

construction cost and value engineering

Based on our initial conversations, the Architect has utilized square foot Unit Costs in determining an Estimated Project Cost for the purposes of this proposal. ZINN ties Unit Costs to the actual costs of recently completed projects. Unit Costs therefore do not always exactly match market costs which can fluctuate dramatically over the course of the Architect’s work. Actual costs for this project will only become available many months or years after this Proposal is developed. Estimates provided by Architect therefore, either verbally or in writing, are no guarantee of Project cost. Any additional work at the behest of the Client to adjust the Project Scope and/or Permit/Bid/Construction Documents in order to change the Project costs will be performed as Additional Services. Actual Project Cost is determined by the general contractor under separate contract.
